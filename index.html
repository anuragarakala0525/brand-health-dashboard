How a Brand Metric score is built
Every metric follows the same pipeline from raw signal to board-ready reporting.
Raw-value policy: Branded Google searches, total engaged site visits and 6Sense distinct high intent account counts are displayed in their original units. They are not presented to clients as normalized /100 proxy scores. A separate internal scaling layer is retained only to keep existing composite roll-ups operational.

Insights from the graph
  • First back-to-back positive months since the Oct-25 peak: Jul-26 (+1.5% MoM) and Aug-26 (+4.2% MoM) are the first two consecutive months of growth in 11 months. Two months is not a confirmed trend — Sep and Oct are historically the highest-volume months (Sep-25: 4.17M, Oct-25: 4.57M), making the next 2 months critical for confirmation.
  • YoY gap is large but narrowing: Aug-26 (2.92M) vs Aug-25 (3.81M) = −23.2% YoY. At the current pace of ~+117K/mo, returning to Aug-25 levels would take approximately 8 months — making Q4 2026/Q1 2027 the earliest realistic recovery window.
  • Root cause is unconfirmed — three hypotheses to test: (a) seasonal demand recovery post-summer, (b) specific content/campaigns driving branded queries, (c) reduced competitor branded activity. Root cause determines the correct briefing — do not assume broad spend increase before knowing which it is.
Recommended actions
1
Investigate competitor capture, reduced spend, and lighter press as separate hypotheses
If non-branded search is flat or growing while branded search falls, separate these as distinct root causes before deciding the response. Do not conflate them into a single brief.
2
Do not add paid media spend until root cause is confirmed
Adding budget before diagnosing the driver risks reinforcing the wrong signal. Hold spend decisions until the cause — competitor capture, campaign gap, or demand shift — is established.
